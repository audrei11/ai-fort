Bakery Company · Jul – Aug 2025

NEXPOS PRO — POS System

Most POS systems stop at the receipt. Sale recorded. Receipt printed. That's it — nobody knows what ingredients were used, nobody knows how much stock is left, and nobody gets alerted when something runs low until it's already gone. Restaurant owners only find out they're out of stock when a customer is already waiting. That's too late.

I built NEXPOS so it never gets to that point. Every sale automatically deducts the exact ingredients used — sell a latte, the milk and espresso are deducted; sell a breakfast meal, the eggs, rice, and protein are deducted. Stock levels stay accurate after every single transaction with zero manual counting. When any ingredient drops below threshold, the system immediately notifies the supervisor, the boss, and the supplier — no one has to check, no one has to make the call.

Optional self-order kiosk lets customers place orders on a touchscreen, flowing automatically to the cashier and kitchen display. Same system. Zero extra manual work. Built for restaurants, bars, cafeterias, and coffee shops.
